IPS Green Vision is a student-focused sustainability platform that turns environmental awareness into practical action.

About the project

Small actions can create meaningful change.

IPS Green Vision was created by Aayann K Jain for Indirapuram Public School. It helps students explore the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals and take simple environmental actions in their daily lives.

The platform brings together action sharing, personal green targets, local tree-planting guidance and city environmental information in one accessible place.

Learn more about the school at ipsindirapuramncr.com.
