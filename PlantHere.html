How It Works

  1. Click "Find Planting Spots Near Me"
  2. Choose your search radius (1–5 km)
  3. The AI scans for parks, open spaces, empty lots, and roadsides near you
  4. Each spot is scored for tree planting suitability
  5. Pick a spot and go plant a tree!

🛰 Data Sources

OpenStreetMap for locations · Google Earth Engine for environmental analysis

🧠 AI Scoring

Each spot is scored based on temperature, rainfall, NDVI, tree cover, elevation, and land type.
